\section{Findings}

Suspended-sediment concentration peaked on the flood tide at every
station, consistent with the shallow-estuary regime
of~\cite{rivers2023}. The mid-reach stations showed the sharpest
gradient, which the handbook attributes to the channel
constriction~\cite{handbook2021}.

\subsection{Next steps}

A second survey is scheduled for autumn to test whether the gradient
persists outside the spring freshet. The sampling design is unchanged
so the two seasons remain directly comparable.
